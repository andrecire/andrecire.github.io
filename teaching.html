My teaching spans mathematical programming, data analytics, and operations management across undergraduate, professional master's, and doctoral levels. Below is a list of my primary courses.

Graduate Courses

Optimizing Supply Chain and Logistics Analytics
Rotman School of Management • Master of Management Analytics (MMA)
Provides optimization skills in supply chain operations. Topics include network design, aggregate planning, routing, and pricing.
Business Data Analytics
University of Toronto Scarborough • Master of Accounting and Finance (MAccFin)
Provides skills in data manipulation, data cleaning, and predictive models emphasizing applications in Accounting and Finance.
Duality in Optimization (Advanced Methods in Linear and Non-linear Optimization)
Rotman School of Management • Ph.D. in Operations Management
Investigates the use of duality in optimization and classical results in the field, including topics in polyhedral analysis, convex analysis, and decomposition methods.

Undergraduate Courses

Introductory/Advanced Business Data Analytics
University of Toronto Scarborough
Provides skills in data manipulation, data cleaning, and predictive models (such as decision trees, random forests, and neural networks) for Management students, emphasizing managerial insights and principles of evidence-based decision making.
Operations Management: A Mathematical Approach
University of Toronto Scarborough
Provides skills in practical quantitative approaches to assess and improve upon decisions underlying the strategic goals of a firm. Topics include project management, forecasting, total quality, supply chain, inventory management, and basics of revenue management.
Analysis for Decision Making
University of Toronto Scarborough
Provides skills in prescriptive analytics models to enhance decision making and analysis of outcomes. Topics include decision analysis, mathematical programming, multi-criteria decision making, and queuing models using Excel.
Mathematical Models for Consulting
Tepper School of Business, Carnegie Mellon University
Provides skills in mathematical programming techniques for decision making, including linear and integer programming.
